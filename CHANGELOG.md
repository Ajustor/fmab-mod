# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-09

### New features

- Transmutation circles drawn in the Notebook, traced with chalk and explained by the Alchemy Treatise
- Multi-stage circles with links and satellites, Fire and Air glyphs, flame jet, gust, smelting and amplified combustion
- Knowledge tree for the Earth and Metal schools, Alchemist rank earned through practice, Recompose glyph, temporary stone lance and arm blade
- Circles on floors, walls and ceilings; lasting alchemical paint and a chisel to engrave stone and metal
- Alchemist gloves embroidered or engraved at the alchemist's table, cast with a key, at range (spark) or by striking (gauntlets)
- Izumi Curtis, her trials rewarded with knowledge, and sparring matches
- Web circle editor running the same parser as the game, to design circles and import them into the notebook
- The circle editor offers the Treatise's example circles, ready to open and change
- Schools of Fire, Ice and Explosion: ice spikes and walls, detonations
- State Alchemist examination against a stone golem bound to its circle, and the State Alchemist's watch
- Embroidered garments and ritual tattoos bearing passive circles; tattooed palms cast with a key
- Central City, a great walled city with its headquarters and the State examiner, and the hamlet of Resembool
- The starting notebook holds simple circles, and the Treatise copies its examples into the notebook in one click
- The notebook shows a coloured verdict and tells which glyph an unreadable stroke resembles
- Glyphs you do not understand work with a risk of rebound and are learned through practice or alchemy tomes; the notebook stamps the glyphs you understand
- Human transmutation opens the Gate of Truth: tolls, soul armor, circleless transmutation, automail, Winry and Rush Valley
- Village librarians sell alchemy tomes, like enchanted books
- Truth wears the parts it steals; a soul without armor wanders before the Gate until a soul circle calls it, and can prepare its own seals
- The soul dwells in a hollow armor, mends it by transmuting its material into it, can change armor, and anyone can trace their seal with ink or blood
- Drawn circles come in sizes (1, 3, 5 or 7 blocks): bigger is stronger and costlier, smaller is more discreet
- Every player has their own blood seal, shown by the Treatise and given to the soul with the circle that mends its armor
- Transmutations unleash the blue lightning of the alchemic reaction
- Lust and Gluttony guard Laboratory 5; homunculi reconstitute while their Stone holds souls, and Gluttony swallows into his Belly
- Envy roams in disguise, reveals himself, turns into a giant monster then a small lizard
- Greed runs the Devil's Nest; his Ultimate Shield only opens when he strikes, and a pact makes him an ally paid by the day
- The Philosopher's Stone, fused from four homunculus cores, gives a soul bound to an armor its body back
- The Philosopher's Stone gives back everything the Gate took, each part costing souls according to its severity
- Sloth charges through his tunnel under Central, Wrath dodges with his Ultimate Eye, and Pride's shadows only live with light
- The Philosopher's Stone amplifies transmutations and is made by sacrifice; karma, the eclipse and the Living Stone arrive
- The Living Stone absorbs souls without limit; keeping a hand on a circle makes the effect grow, and transmutation gestures are animated
- Truth grows familiar with each visit, mocks bodiless souls and offers them a fresh start (configurable in config/fmab.json)
- Transmutation gestures on the player model: palms on the circle, held hands, clapped hands, arm reaching for a soul
- Father, the original Homunculus, beneath Central: three-form boss, anti-alchemy zone and Ouroboros seal
- Xing alkahestry: five kunai in a circle heal or trap, even near Father; May Chang teaches it
- Fort Briggs, General Olivier Armstrong, the Briggs sabre (×1.5 against homunculi) and Drachman soldiers
- Alchemist's path advancements, cartographer maps to the mod's places, and the national circle blood crest quest
- Barry the Chopper, Amestrian and Briggs soldiers, haunted armors, immortal soldiers and chimeras
- Scar's arm, Kimblee's mines, Hawkeye's pistol and rifle, Hughes's knives, the Xing sword; Mustang's gloves fail in the rain
- Liore and Cornello, the Ishval ruins and Scar, the Xerxes ruins and Hohenheim, Yock Island and Izumi's last trial
- Cinematics for the Gate (black arms, the eye and the flood of knowledge), the Promised Day and Father's fall; the mod's own sounds; sneak to absorb souls
- Dublith and the Curtis butcher shop, where Izumi lives; she hands out the map to Yock Island
- Gold glyph: turn iron into gold, forbidden by State law (karma, soldiers on your heels)
- Triggers for inscribed circles: delayed, trap, or redstone signal
- Schools of Destruction, Medicine (alkahestry) and Life; craft chimeras that obey you
- Amestrian bricks, Xing bricks and Briggs packed snow, in structures and craftable
- Doctor Marcoh and his clinic; his ciphered notes, disguised as recipes, which he turns back into alchemy tomes
- Black arms drag wandering souls back to their Gate; two alchemists can fuse their circles
- The eclipse shows in the sky: the moon in front of the sun, a dark red sky and stars at noon
- Distinct silhouettes for Gluttony (belly), Sloth (bulk) and Father (beard and mane)
- Silhouettes for Lust (long hair), Envy (palm-tree spikes) and Greed (fur collar)
- Silhouettes for Wrath (coat), Pride (shadows) and Barry (crest); Fort Briggs and Yock Island better placed
- Circle notebook on the N key, circle wheel (R) to switch circles in combat, copying unknown circles, very rare Notes on Human Transmutation
- Towns are lived in and furnished, guards at Central's gates; boss bars only show in combat; no more places inside Central
- Top-left panel: selected circle and body status (automail and its wear, lost limbs)
- Body tab in the inventory (limbs, automail, gloves, organs, sight); the selected circle only shows when it can be used

### Fixes

- Glyphs and combinations now load in game: the Treatise and notebook circles work
- Many v1 fixes: black arms, cinematics, the Gate, Fort Briggs, recipes, Scar, homunculus kill credit, balance, place generation
- Fixes: houses can finally be entered, Izumi's sparring, an eclipse that darkens the world, bound chimeras, traps and armed circles
- Fixes: Izumi and Marcoh stay home, Fort Briggs sits properly, gradual eclipse, disguised Envy true to its victim
- Before testing: playable without a right arm, chestplate seals, concentration by rank, reachable places, dodgeable Father sun, maps handed out, recipes known
- A full notebook saves without disconnecting; sealed blood crests survive the update
- The game no longer crashes on launch; the soul chestplate enchants like iron
