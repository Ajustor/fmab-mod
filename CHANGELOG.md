# Changelog

## 1.12.0 — 2026-10-23

### New features

- On eclipse day, the moon crosses the sky: pale at sunrise, it reaches the sun, bites into it around ten, hides it from eleven to two leaving only a corona, then moves away; the light dims as more of the sun is covered.
- Alchemy becomes a tool: the woodcutter fells the trees around (Wood and Decompose), the harvest reaps and replants (Plant and Decompose), the fertiliser makes crops grow (Plant and Project), the tunnel bores through stone (Earth and Decompose with an arrow, or on a wall), the bridge spans the void and earthworks level the ground (Earth and Air fused); five new Treatise pages show them.
## 1.11.1 — 2026-10-23

### Fixes

- Gloves now show in first person, no longer vanish under long sleeves or on slim arms, and show the circle inscribed in them.
## 1.11.0 — 2026-10-22

### New features

- The right circle repairs everything an anvil repairs: Wood, Earth, Copper, Iron, Gold, Netherite, Carbon (diamond), Fiber (leather), Flesh (elytra) or Lime (scute), with Repair and the item's material.
- Homunculi (Selim included) only go after those who threaten Father's plans: whoever strikes them, carries a living Stone, refused Father's offer, saw one of them fall or sealed a blood crest.
- The circle wheel holds nine circles per wheel: scroll to go from one wheel to the next (an empty one at the end to start another), and hold right click to put a circle wherever you like.

### Fixes

- Monsters no longer spawn in towns and villages (Central, Dublith, Resembool, Rush Valley, Liore, Xing, Marcoh's clinic), and their streets are better lit.
## 1.10.3 — 2026-10-21

### Fixes

- Cartographers no longer freeze the server while preparing their maps to the mod's places (Dublith, Resembool…); those maps, like the characters' ones, lead to the place without searching the world.
## 1.10.2 — 2026-10-21

### New features

- The notebook holds 32 circles and gains a contents page (click the page number); past 16 circles, the wheel lays them out on two rings.

### Fixes

- With Distant Horizons, the distant view of the mod's dimensions no longer runs world generation in the background, which could freeze world saving.
## 1.10.1 — 2026-10-21

### Fixes

- Izumi: the two-stage circle trial is marked for Alchemists, a new trial (understand a glyph by drawing it) is within an Apprentice's reach, and hovering over a trial tells you how to pass it.
- The two-stage circle trial moves from Izumi to the State examination, where it is required before the items; Izumi's trials are all within an Apprentice's reach.
## 1.10.0 — 2026-10-20

### New features

- The Treatise's chapters unveil themselves as you progress (rank or mastery), and three new chapters teach how to design your own circles: Thinking a circle through, Thinking in stages, Master circles.
- The Treatise takes beginners by the hand: a First steps chapter for the first transmutation, Everyday circles to mine, cross, defend and run redstone, and long texts carry on to the next page instead of being cut off.

### Fixes

- Town houses get a step in front of their door, Dublith, Rush Valley, Resembool and Central are roomier, Liore gains four planted courtyards, and its barrels are no longer empty.
## 1.9.1 — 2026-10-20

### Fixes

- The knowledge tree no longer overflows the screen: schools are laid out in a grid and the list scrolls with the mouse wheel.
## 1.9.0 — 2026-10-18

### New features

- The more you practise a school of alchemy, the less concentration its transmutations cost (down to half).
- New players are welcomed: a greeting, a "First steps" page in the Treatise, the State exam and crystallized blood explained, and Izumi pointed out early.
- The Treatise's glyph catalogue now fills in as you learn: an unknown glyph stays a "???" page telling which tome teaches it and where to find it.
- New mid-game advancements (notebook, tomes, knowledge, gloves, tattoo, State watch, each of Izumi's trials, chimera, gold), and the Alchemist rank is granted as soon as mastery allows.
- A real ending: the Promised Day raises immortal soldiers and weakens alchemy away from sealed blood crests; after Father's fall, Hohenheim says farewell, an epilogue tells what comes next, and his engraved watch goes to the player along with "The End" advancement.
- Bosses now drop real loot (Stone shards, crystallized blood, fitting items), and places have chests filled by data-pack loot tables, including Central's library, the Bradley residence, Sloth's tunnel, Father's lair, Yock Island, blood crests and houses.
- Twelve story characters join the world, with their lines and shops: Mustang, Hawkeye, Armstrong, Hughes, Ling, Lan Fan, Alphonse, Pinako, Basque Grand, McDougal, the Rush Valley tattooist and Shou Tucker.
- Two bosses: Kimblee in Ishval (explosive marks) and the Slicer brothers in Laboratory 5 (two seals to erase); and Shou Tucker's secret, told in a cinematic.
- Ishvalans live in the Ishval ruins and trade their knowledge and goods; the Gate of Truth now stands as a monument in the White Space.
- Drachma gets its places: pillager camps in the snow, and a fort guarded by the Drachman General, a new boss.
- Xing gets its own biome (bamboo, vivid grass, turquoise water) around the pavilion, plus a temple and two houses.
- Water + Air + Fix raises a blinding mist (instead of the ice wall), and copper works with redstone: Fix powers a circuit, Project cuts it.
- Without a circle, spark gloves snap a burst of fire at range and gauntlets raise a line of spikes, like Mustang and Armstrong.
- Four modifier glyphs to place on the ring: circle or cone shape, duration and number of targets (taught by the Forms tome).
- Polish: gloves and tattoos visible on players, one sound per alchemy school, ambience for the White Space and the Belly, completed creative tab and recipe book, and the shell key limited to one press per second.
- A Philosopher's Stone can give a Gate back to a player who gave theirs away: 100 souls by default, configurable, and they start over.

### Fixes

- Story places (Central and Father's lair, Resembool, Briggs, Xing…) now exist only once per world; maps and /locate lead to them.
- Homunculi now spare Father's recruits, unless they strike first.
- The advancement tree now follows the game's actual order: no more advancements shown out of reach right after earning them.
- The Philosopher's Stone now costs souls on every use (rebounds, empty attempts and prolongations included), and the living Stone no longer skips cooldowns or costs.
- Glove tooltips now tell how many stages your rank holds before the circle loses stability (State gloves hold only three for a State Alchemist).
- Balance: gold is capped at one block per transmutation, detonation consumes sulfur, Scar's arm costs by hardness, and chimeras need Life knowledge (three at most).
- Alchemy no longer hurts its caster or their allies (pets, chimeras, teammates, villagers), its kills are credited to the player, and lost sight prevents sprinting.
- Circle cost now scales with range, the transmuted blade comes sharpened and reinforced, and the stone lance no longer turns dirt into deepslate (and can be drawn from the ground again).
## 1.8.0 — 2026-10-17

### New features

- An alchemist who has seen their Gate can sacrifice a limb, through a human transmutation under an armour, to bind another player's soul to it; both accept on screen. By default, this is now the only way to bind someone else's soul (configurable).
- An alchemist who has seen their Gate can give an ally back a lost limb, sight or organs through a human transmutation the ally stands in: they pay with their own soul (and wake up in an armour) or with souls from their Stone; both accept on screen.
- A soul in armour can get its body back: an ally pays with their soul, their Stone's souls, or their Gate, like Edward, and then never performs alchemy again.

### Fixes

- An inhabited armour without leggings now moves like a legless player (slower, no jumping, crawling) until leggings are put back on.
## 1.7.0 — 2026-10-17

### New features

- The mod is available for Minecraft 26.3 as well as 26.2: each version has its own jar.
## 1.6.0 — 2026-10-16

### New features

- A living Philosopher's Stone transmutes without waiting: gloves, gauntlets and Scar's arm have no delay, its alkahestry acts as soon as it is drawn, a channelled work reaches full size at once, and Scar's arm, alkahestry and Kimblee's seals no longer cost it concentration
- A living Philosopher's Stone breaks down projectiles aimed at it before they hit (firearm bullets included) and receives their matter through equivalent exchange

### Fixes

- Treatise, web editor, README and site updated: tomes, detonation, suggested rank, detailed incomplete formula
- The server rate-limits glove casts (two per second) and design page requests; dead players and spectators can no longer cast with their gloves
- The Truth's bargain no longer floods the chat, can actually be left, closes after two minutes and resumes when reconnecting mid-visit
- The Philosopher's Stone choice no longer goes through if the alchemist died or their circle was erased in the meantime
- A drifting soul keeps its place in the queue across disconnects and server restarts
- With the daylight cycle frozen, the eclipse and the Promised Day are no longer announced every tick
- In single player, switching worlds no longer carries over transmutation lightning or passives computed for the previous world; simultaneous lightning is capped

### Performance

- Item compositions are computed at server start and after /reload instead of on the first transmutation; exchange values are cached

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-11

### New features

- Redrawn HUD: the soul counter shows the Stone's icon, concentration a framed gauge, lost sight a soft veil
- Tucker's chimeras get bodies of their own: lion, wolf and snake for the beast, six stitched limbs for the crawler.
- Liore becomes a real desert town: walls, temple avenue, fountain square, covered market and some twenty houses with roof terraces.
- Transmutation gestures now show in first person too: hands clap in front of you, plunge onto the circle or reach for a soul.
- Paths now link the houses of Resembool, Dublith and Rush Valley to their square, lined with lamp posts.
- Making Greed an ally is enough for "The Seven Sins", and new advancements reward the pact with Greed, the Ishvalan tattoo, exposing Cornello, Marcoh's notes, Hohenheim and Marcoh's clinic.
- The State watch, the Stone core, crystallized blood, thread, ink and cartridges have a tooltip explaining what they are for.
- With very low karma, Lust first comes to make you Father's offer: accepting ends the hunts, refusing starts them.
- Barry the Chopper has his own mechanics: a telegraphed cleaver sweep, a charge once wounded, and a seal that holds the first time he falls.
- Test commands /fmab karma [value] and /fmab offer (operators).

### Fixes

- After switching worlds in single player, Scar's arm and the Stone's bargain no longer get stuck; per-player state no longer piles up on a server
- The Treatise contents no longer cut chapter titles; Winry shows each automail's wear as a bar; the gloves screen is easier to read
- Worn circles (embroidery, tattoos) no longer stack the same effect beyond three, and fall silent in Father's zone.
- Envy no longer haunts those who defeated him, wandering homunculi leave when nobody comes near, and only the first of each kind leaves a Stone core.
- Boss difficulty now follows the progression order: Gluttony and Sloth get easier, Pride, Wrath and above all Father become the toughest.
- Circles no longer vanish during the Gate cinematic, and many circles on screen no longer make the game stutter.
- The pistol fires slower, its bullets weaken with distance and barely slow homunculi down.
- Unmasked Pride hunts you and flees to the dusk when his shadows die; his shadows no longer pass through walls and spare your chimeras, your animals and villagers.
- The State exam's transmuted weapon no longer crumbles near the examiner, and casting a weapon without the required knowledge names it and how to get it.
- A soul that opens the Gate again comes back in its own armor, battered, instead of losing it and having to leave a defenseless armor waiting.

### Performance

- Less network traffic for players with lost limbs, automail or worn passives: their attributes are no longer resent every tick
- Player rendering is lighter: no more per-frame search for the pushed wheelchair or rebuilding of the body.
- Recharging concentration no longer sends all the alchemist data to the client every second.
